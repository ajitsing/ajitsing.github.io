FAQ

Is a 12-character password strong enough?

Twelve characters with mixed uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols is a widely used baseline for everyday accounts when longer lengths are not required.

Can I include spaces or symbols?

Yes. Turn on Symbols and optionally Spaces. Spaces count toward the 12-character total. Exclude ambiguous characters if you want something easier to read aloud.

Is my password uploaded?

No. Generation uses crypto.getRandomValues in your browser and never leaves your device.
